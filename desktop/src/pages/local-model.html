This checks the model on a short sample; actual chat and document tasks can take substantially longer. Measurements stay on this Mac and don't establish accuracy on your documents or compare this model with paid legal products.

Build toward your firm's own AI
  1. Start free. Use the local model and add your instructions and documents. Keep a record of answers you correct.
  2. Measure your work. Assemble reviewed examples with expected answers, citations and access requirements. Keep a separate evaluation set.
  3. Choose the next investment. Improve document retrieval and instructions first. If measurements show a gap, evaluate a larger model, a firm-owned private deployment, or supervised fine-tuning with our help.

Conversations do not automatically train the model. Training should use examples your firm has reviewed and approved. This release runs base models; deployment and tuning services are the next guided step.
